Wiring 

1602 LCD with IC2 --> Arduino UNO 
GND --> GND
VCC --> 5V
SDA --> Analog In A4
SCL --> Analog In A5

Rain Drop Sensor --> Arduino UNO 
S (Analog out) --> Analog In A0
+ (VCC) --> 5V 
- (GND) --> GND
